What did Jesus say about discipleship?

Jesus’ calls to discipleship range from two words—“Follow me”—to demanding teachings about self-denial, loyalty, endurance, love, and fruitfulness. The sayings resist reducing discipleship to a single decision; they describe an ongoing way of life.

Published Aug. 14, 2026 · Every Word of Jesus editorial project · How this guide was built

At a glance

  • The first call is personal and active: follow Jesus.
  • Following includes self-denial and taking up the cross, not merely admiration.
  • Jesus connects true discipleship with continuing in his word.
  • Luke 14 stresses counting the cost rather than beginning casually.
  • John connects discipleship with love, abiding, obedience, and fruit.

Follow me

The early calling scenes are strikingly direct. Jesus calls people out of ordinary work and into a new vocation shaped around himself.

“Come ye after me, and I will make you to become fishers of men. Follow me.”
Matthew 4:19; Mark 1:17

Deny yourself and take up the cross

After Peter’s confession and the first passion prediction, Jesus describes following in terms of self-denial, the cross, and losing one’s life for his sake.

“If any man will come after me ... let him deny himself, and take up his cross daily, and follow me.”
Matthew 16:24; Mark 8:34; Luke 9:23

Continue in my word

In John 8, discipleship is tied to remaining in Jesus’ teaching. The promise of knowing the truth follows that continuing relationship.

“If ye continue in my word, then are ye my disciples indeed; and ye shall know the truth, and the truth shall make you free.”
John 8:31–32

Count the cost

Luke records unusually severe language about competing loyalties. Jesus follows it with illustrations about a tower and a king preparing for war: a disciple should understand the cost before claiming the path.

“Whosoever doth not bear his cross, and come after me, cannot be my disciple.”
Luke 14:27

Known by love

At the Last Supper, the identity of Jesus’ disciples is not tied to status or visibility but to the love they show one another.

“By this shall all men know that ye are my disciples, if ye have love one to another.”
John 13:35
