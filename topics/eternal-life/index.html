What did Jesus say about eternal life?

“Eternal life” and “everlasting life” appear repeatedly in sayings attributed to Jesus, especially in John. The theme includes present possession, future resurrection, relationship with God, and the promise that death does not have the final word.

Published Aug. 14, 2026 · Every Word of Jesus editorial project · How this guide was built

At a glance

  • John’s Gospel repeatedly links eternal life with believing in the Son and hearing his word.
  • Jesus describes eternal life as both a present reality and a future resurrection hope.
  • The Good Shepherd saying emphasizes hearing, following, security, and life given by Jesus.
  • In Jesus’ prayer of John 17, eternal life is described relationally: knowing the Father and the one he sent.

Belief and everlasting life

In the conversation with Nicodemus, the lifted serpent becomes an image for the Son of man being lifted up. The passage then connects belief with everlasting life and salvation.

“Whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have everlasting life.”
John 3:15–16

Passed from death unto life

In John 5, Jesus speaks of hearing his word and believing the Father who sent him. Eternal life is described in the present tense even as the discourse also looks toward resurrection.

“He that heareth my word, and believeth on him that sent me, hath everlasting life ... but is passed from death unto life.”
John 5:24

The bread of life

After the feeding of the multitude, Jesus repeatedly contrasts perishing food with the life he gives. The discourse points to resurrection “at the last day.”

“Every one which seeth the Son, and believeth on him, may have everlasting life: and I will raise him up at the last day.”
John 6:40

The Shepherd gives eternal life

The sheep hear the Shepherd’s voice and follow him. The promise combines relationship, security, and life.

“My sheep hear my voice, and I know them, and they follow me: and I give unto them eternal life; and they shall never perish.”
John 10:27–28

Life eternal is knowing God

In the prayer of John 17, Jesus gives one of the New Testament’s most compact descriptions of eternal life.

“And this is life eternal, that they might know thee the only true God, and Jesus Christ, whom thou hast sent.”
John 17:3
