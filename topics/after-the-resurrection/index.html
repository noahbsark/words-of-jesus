Topics / After the resurrection

What did Jesus say after the resurrection?

Read Jesus’ words at the tomb, on the road and with the disciples. Follow the instructions he gives before the ascension, then see how Acts and Revelation present later encounters.

At the tomb and with the disciplesInstructions before the ascensionLater accounts and visions

The readings are grouped by setting. Follow each account’s own sequence as you read the full chapter.

At the tomb and with the disciples

Jesus greets the women

Start after the women leave the tomb. Matthew places Jesus’ greeting before his instruction that the disciples go to Galilee.

Matthew 28:8–10Find the reader excerpt

Jesus speaks to Mary Magdalene

John records Mary’s first answer before she recognizes Jesus. Notice what changes when he calls her by name and what message he asks her to take to the disciples.

John 20:11–18Find the reader excerpt

The conversation on the road to Emmaus

Read the travelers’ account of what they had hoped before Jesus replies. Luke summarizes Jesus’ explanation of Scripture, then places recognition later at the table.

Luke 24:13–35Find the reader excerpt

Peace and recognition

Luke records the disciples’ fear before Jesus speaks about his hands and feet. John records the greeting behind shut doors, then returns to the scene eight days later with Thomas present.

Instructions before the ascension

A commission in Galilee

Matthew begins the scene with the disciples’ response to seeing Jesus. Follow the movement from his authority to the task of making disciples and the final promise.

Matthew 28:16–20Find the reader excerpt

Witnesses in Luke’s closing chapter

Luke connects Jesus’ explanation of Scripture with a message of repentance and forgiveness. The disciples receive instructions to stay in the city until they are given power.

Luke 24:44–53Find the reader excerpt

Jesus and Peter by the sea

Follow the three questions and the work Jesus gives Peter each time. Read the final exchange and the narrator’s clarification about what Jesus said concerning the other disciple.

Waiting and bearing witness

In Acts, the disciples ask about restoring the kingdom to Israel. Jesus answers about times and then describes their work as witnesses, from Jerusalem outward.

Later accounts and visions

Jesus addresses Saul

Acts narrates the encounter on the road and later records two retellings by Paul. Read each account before combining its questions or instructions.
