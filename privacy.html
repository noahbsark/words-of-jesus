Privacy policy

Reading does not require an account. The sayings search, saved items and translation preferences work on your device.

Last updated: November 12, 2026.

Saved sayings and preferences

Your browser’s local storage remembers saved sayings, display theme and Bible translation. These values are not sent to us as analytics events. Clear site data in your browser to remove them. Other users of the same browser profile may be able to see them.

Searches and reading addresses

The site does not load Google Analytics or send search terms, saved saying IDs or religious reading interests to an analytics service. Filters and passage references may appear in page addresses so you can return or share them. A shared URL can reveal what you were reading. Hosting and security providers may receive requested URLs and standard technical information such as IP address, browser details and timestamps to deliver and protect the site.

Hosting

Cloudflare Pages hosts and delivers the website. GitHub stores the public source code and revision history. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy for its processing practices.

Advertising

The site is applying to Google AdSense. When advertising is enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies, identifiers and technical data to serve and measure ads, subject to consent choices where required. See how Google uses information from partner sites and Google’s advertising controls. Browser cookie controls are separate from an advertising consent message.

External links and affiliate links

External Bible services and retailers apply their own privacy policies. Some resource links are Amazon affiliate links; a qualifying purchase may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Opening one shares standard request and referral information with that service.

Contact

For questions or corrections use the contact page. If you email us, your address and the contents of the message are processed to respond. Please do not include sensitive personal information.
