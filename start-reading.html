Start reading Jesus’ words

  1. Hear the opening message

    Mark begins Jesus’ public teaching with an announcement and then shows him calling four fishermen. Read what he asks people to do in verse 15 before watching how the fishermen respond.

    As you read: What changes when the message becomes an invitation to follow?

    Read Mark 1:14–20Next reading

  2. Listen to the blessings

    Start with who gathers around Jesus. As the blessings unfold, notice the shift from describing groups of people to speaking directly to the listeners.

    As you read: Where does Jesus change from “they” to “you,” and what does he say next?

    Read Matthew 5:1–12Next reading

  3. Read a prayer in its setting

    Before the prayer, Jesus contrasts ways of praying and says the Father knows what people need. The verses immediately after the prayer return to one of its requests.

    As you read: Which request does Jesus explain again after the prayer?

    Read Matthew 6:5–15Next reading

  4. Follow a question into a story

    A lawyer’s questions lead to the story of the Samaritan. Keep track of who asks the first question about a neighbor and who answers the last one.

    As you read: How does Jesus change the question by the end of the story?

    Read Luke 10:25–37Next reading

  5. Read what Jesus does before he speaks

    Peter objects when Jesus comes to wash his feet. Read the exchange and then notice the change when Jesus sits down and asks the whole group what he has done.

    As you read: How does the example make sense of the instruction that follows?

    Read John 13:1–17Next reading

  6. Stay with the conversation after the resurrection

    The travelers explain what they had expected before Jesus answers. Luke summarizes his explanation of Scripture and describes their recognition later, around a meal.

    As you read: What do the travelers say about the conversation after they recognize him?
